User guide

Basic operating guide for dispatchers, instructors, and admin users working in the AOA Operations Hub.

Quick start Home Students SAP Matrix Master schedule Ground trainers Operations notes Tracking pages Management
Access

Quick start

  1. Log in with your staff account. New accounts must be approved by an admin before the hub opens.
  2. Use the top navigation to move between dispatch tools. Admin-only controls appear only for admin users.
  3. Watch the save status labels on scheduling and management pages before leaving after a change.
Dispatch

Home

The home page is the operational heads-up board. Use it first during a shift to see student items, notes, weather, and live operations.

  • Spin training events: shows students with spin training due in the next 7 days. Use + Add to choose a roster student, date, and time.
  • Completed: clears the active spin training alert and saves the completion to the student profile.
  • Student watch: highlights active students with urgent issues such as low attendance, no weekly sessions, missing CFI, stale updates, or time off.
  • Latest notes: shows current staff communication, with pinned notes at the top.
Roster

Students

  • Use the student roster to search, filter, and open profile cards.
  • On a profile, use Include on attendance list to hide students from MyFBO attendance screens when they are visiting from another campus or should not be tracked locally.
  • Use Spin training event, date, and time when a student should appear on the home spin watch.
  • The profile records the last spin training completion when dispatch marks it completed from the home page.
  • CSV import/export includes attendance list and spin training fields.
Admin

SAP Matrix timelines

  1. Open a student profile and select Upload SAP Matrix, or open SAP Timeline from the Students tabs.
  2. Upload the PDF and wait for the server to validate and extract it. PandaDoc signature certificate pages are ignored.
  3. Review the proposed student match, profile values, milestone types, projected dates, and linked written/practice/stage-check status.
  4. Correct, skip, keep, replace, or add rows as needed, then confirm. Uploading alone never changes the live timeline.
  5. Use the SAP dashboard for overdue items, due windows, written readiness, unscheduled checks, endorsements, and projected graduation risk.

Only administrators can upload, confirm, or open source SAP PDFs. Actual completions, results, and instructor notes are preserved when projected dates change.

Scheduling

Master schedule

  • New booking: create a single student or activity booking with instructor, aircraft, day, start, and end time.
  • Batch schedule: create repeated bookings across selected days.
  • Batch delete: remove multiple bookings by student/activity, instructor, day, or category.
  • Instructor by day: plan each instructor row across the week.
  • Daily by airplane: view aircraft capacity. Items with no available airplane appear in the overbooked row.
  • Drag and drop: admin users can move bookings to another time, instructor row, or aircraft row.
Ground

Ground trainers and attendance

  • Use Ground Trainers to book weekly trainer slots.
  • Use Ground Attendance to create ground school sessions and track check-ins.
  • Students hidden from the attendance list are excluded from the MyFBO attendance tracking view.
Communication

Operations notes

  • Post signed notes for dispatch handoffs, schedule reminders, or policy updates.
  • Pin high-priority notes so they stay at the top of the notes list and home page.
  • Use search to find older notes by text or author.
Trackers

Maintenance, oil, fuel, E6B, tuition, written tests, and stage checks

  • Fleet Maintenance: opens the maintenance tracker preview and full maintenance dashboard.
  • Oil Tracker: records oil added by aircraft, tach time, date, and initials.
  • Fuel Reports: logs fueling delays, contamination, fuel levels, and related notes.
  • E6B Rentals: tracks calculator rentals and returns.
  • Tuition Tracking: imports and tracks payment schedule details.
  • Written Test Tracking: tracks written test progress and scheduled tests.
  • Stage Checks: monitors stage check request and scheduling timing.
Admin

Management

  • The Management page is visible to admin users.
  • Staff accounts: approve users, update names/initials, grant or remove admin access, and send reset emails.
  • Aircraft: add or remove aircraft from the Master Schedule aircraft list.
  • When an aircraft is removed, existing bookings using that aircraft are moved to another open aircraft at the same time when possible.
  • If no aircraft is open, the booking is left with no aircraft and will appear as no plane available / overbooked in the airplane schedule view.
